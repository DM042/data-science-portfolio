## Project Showcase

### Heart Disease Risk Factor Analysis
- **Status:** Completed
- **Research Question:** Which clinical features are most strongly associated with the presence of heart disease?

#### Visualization & Findings
![Heart Disease Rate by Chest Pain Type](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DM042/data-science-project/main/fig1_chest_pain.png)

**Brief Explanation of Findings:**
1. Heart disease rates vary greatly by chest pain type, with the **Asymptomatic (ASY)** group having the highest rate.
2. The overall **Maximum Heart Rate (MaxHR)** in the heart disease group is lower than in the no-disease group.
3. *Limitation:* Because this public dataset has a limited sample size and some duplicate records, the results are for demonstration only and should not be used for real clinical decisions.
